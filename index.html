Most HRIS audits fail for the same 4 reasons

01

No demand governance

Everything is urgent. Nothing is prioritized by rule. Whoever escalates loudest gets the work done.

02

No ownership model

HR, IT, and the vendor all own part of the system. Which means no one truly owns it. Decisions stall.

03

Configuration without control

"Just make the change." No design authority. No standards. Security sprawl and reporting breaks follow.

04

No system of orchestration

Workday. JSM. SharePoint. Email. They all exist. None of them connect. Fragmentation, not a system.

Built on the PDC Control Model

Every control in your HRIS environment falls into one of three categories. The Playbook maps all of them.

🛡

Prevent

Stop problems before they happen. Governance frameworks, access controls, configuration standards, and design authority.

🔍

Detect

Find problems that slip through. Audit triggers, reconciliation cycles, data quality checks, and exception monitoring.

🔧

Correct

Fix what's broken, systematically. Remediation playbooks, escalation paths, control-failure decision trees, and RACI clarity.

8 Audit Domains. Complete Coverage.

Each domain includes a maturity rubric, control checklist, PDC mapping, and remediation actions.

Domain 01

Data Governance

Ownership rules, entry standards, correction triggers, and master data integrity.

Domain 02

Access & Security

Role-based access, review cadence, segregation of duties, and privilege escalation controls.

Domain 03

Configuration Management

Domain 04

Integration & Interfaces

Domain 05

Report ownership, calculated field governance, dashboard standards, and data lineage.

Domain 06

Demand & Intake

Triage rules, prioritisation logic, SLA frameworks, and service catalogue governance.

Domain 07

Compliance & Audit

Audit log triggers, regulatory mapping, SOX readiness, and control testing schedules.

Domain 08

Operating Model

RACI clarity, team structures, vendor management, and continuous improvement cadence.
